Frequently Asked Questions

You can log in using your official student number and portal password. If you forget your credentials, reset them via the password recovery option or visit the IT Services desk.

Subject adjustments must be submitted within the first two weeks of the semester. Secure the Add/Drop Form from the Registrar’s office and obtain your department adviser's approval signature.

Payments can be made directly at the campus cashier window or online through authorized banking partners and payment channels listed in the portal finance tab.

Requests for Transcripts of Records (TOR), Certificates of Grades (COG), or Enrollment Certificates should be filed online through the Student Portal services tab or in person at the Registrar.

Students missing an exam due to illness or emergency must submit an excuse letter accompanied by a valid medical certificate to their professor within three days to qualify for a special exam.
