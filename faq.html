Frequently Asked Questions

Is Computer Engineering more on hardware or software?

Computer Engineering bridges both worlds! It covers hardware design (circuits, microprocessors, digital logic) alongside low-level to high-level software development (embedded C/C++, firmware, systems programming, and application development).

What is the difference between Computer Engineering (CpE) and Computer Science (CS)?

Computer Engineering emphasizes how hardware and software integrate, focusing on microprocessors, embedded devices, circuit design, and physical computing. Computer Science focuses more on software engineering, algorithms, data structures, and theoretical computation.

What programming languages will I learn in Computer Engineering?

You typically work with low-level languages like Assembly and C/C++ for hardware control and embedded systems, alongside higher-level languages such as Python, Java, or JavaScript for software development and web applications.

What career paths are available for Computer Engineering graduates?

Graduates can pursue roles such as Embedded Systems Engineer, Firmware Developer, Software Engineer, Network Administrator/Engineer, Systems Analyst, IoT Specialist, or Hardware Design Engineer.

Do I need advanced math and circuit knowledge to succeed in CpE?

Yes, foundational mathematics (calculus, discrete math, linear algebra) and basic electrical circuit analysis are essential for understanding digital logic, signal processing, and computer architecture.
